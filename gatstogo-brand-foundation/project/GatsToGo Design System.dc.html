BRAND FOUNDATION & DESIGN SYSTEM

GatsToGo

A pay-before-you-fill system for LPG plants. Customers pay, get a code, get a receipt with the exact weight delivered. Staff authorise fills on a tablet. Owners reconcile daily takings. Built to read cleanly in bright sun, on cheap screens, for owners in their 40s and 50s who want a dependable tool — not a startup toy.
